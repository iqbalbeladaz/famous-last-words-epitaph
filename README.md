## The entry

> Here lies the man who asked, 'What could possibly go wrong?' — This.

One line, 68 characters (gate: 1–150).

## Why this line should win

- **Unmistakably an epitaph of famous last words:** "Here lies" is the
  stone-carver's opening, and "What could possibly go wrong?" is the single
  most famous last-words phrase there is — the rhetorical question everyone
  asks right before disaster.
- **The joke lands on first read:** setup (his dying question) and turn
  (the answer) arrive in one breath, with no explanation needed.
- **The twist is the tombstone itself:** "— This." makes the stone you are
  reading the answer to his question. The grave is the punchline.
- **Reads like something carved in stone:** one short line, no stage
  directions, no explanation — just the epitaph and its sting.
- **Stays in the head:** short, quotable, and repeatable to a friend —
  the kind of line that gets retold at the pub.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. It is fully
deterministic: the same bytes on every run, no input, no randomness, no
network.
